# Natal Orbit Support

**I don't know my birth time**
Tick "I don't know my birth time" on the second step. Natal Orbit uses noon for planet positions and leaves out the Rising sign and houses rather than guessing them. The Moon's sign is marked if it changed that day.

**My city isn't in the list**
Pick the nearest large city. A few dozen miles changes the chart by less than a degree.

**The Rising sign looks different from another app**
Rising depends on an exact time and place. Check the time, AM/PM and city. Natal Orbit uses the historical time zone and daylight-saving rules for your birth date.

**Placidus or Whole Sign?**
Tap the house-system name above your chart to switch. Whole Sign puts each sign in one house starting from your Rising sign; Placidus divides houses by time. Near the poles Natal Orbit falls back to Porphyry houses.

**"Today" doesn't match my horoscope**
Natal Orbit reads real transits against your own chart, not Sun-sign columns, so it will often differ.

**Share card didn't save**
Allow Photos access when iOS asks, or use the share sheet to send it instead.

**Start over**
Tap "New chart" at the bottom and tick "This is my own chart" to replace the saved one.
