# Natal Orbit Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Natal Orbit draws and reads your birth chart. It is built to collect nothing.

**What Natal Orbit does**
- Calculates your chart on your iPhone from the birth date, time and city you type in.
- Stores your chart, any charts you add to compare, and your house-system choice on your iPhone only.
- Makes share cards you can save or send with the iOS share sheet; nothing is uploaded by the app.

**What Natal Orbit does not do**
- No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No servers: your birth data never leaves your iPhone. The app works fully offline.

**Deleting your data**
Tap "New chart" to replace your chart, or delete the app. Everything Natal Orbit stored is removed.
