# Pulse Lab Support

**What do I need?**
An iPhone with Apple Health data from your watch. WHOOP is optional: connect it in Settings (a WHOOP membership is needed), or log WHOOP numbers by hand.

**A chart is empty**
- Open Settings › Data check. It shows, metric by metric, what is arriving from Apple Health and from WHOOP.
- Apple Health: iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Pulse Lab › Turn On All.
- Some metrics (wrist temperature, blood oxygen, HRV) only exist if your watch records them.

**How is my Watch readiness worked out?**
Tap the readiness score at the top of Today. It shows every contributor with today's value, your baseline, the difference and the points it added or took away, what's missing, a 30-day trend and how it compares with WHOOP recovery. Readiness is a general wellness estimate, not medical advice.

**Where do I see more about one number?**
Tap any tile or head-to-head row on Today (or any metric in All data) to open its page: the latest value and its source, your normal range on a chart, low, average and high, the trend against last week and how closely the two trackers agree.

**WHOOP won't connect**
Make sure you can sign in at whoop.com, then try Settings › Connect WHOOP again. Disconnect and reconnect if numbers stop updating.

**Supplements in Apple Health**
Checking off a supplement in your Stack saves its nutrients to Apple Health. Unchecking it removes them.

**Pro, subscriptions and restore**
Settings › Pulse Lab Pro shows the plans, Restore and Manage Subscription. Cancel anytime in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.
