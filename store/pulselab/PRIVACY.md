# Pulse Lab Privacy Policy

Pulse Lab compares your Apple Watch data (from Apple Health) with your WHOOP data (from the WHOOP API).

- **Your data stays on your iPhone.** Pulse Lab has no account, no servers and no analytics.
- **Apple Health:** read only. Pulse Lab never writes to Apple Health and never shares Health data.
- **WHOOP:** after you sign in with WHOOP, Pulse Lab downloads your profile, body measurements, cycles, recovery, sleep and workouts directly from WHOOP to your phone. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone Keychain. Tap Disconnect in Settings to remove them.
- **Exports:** a CSV export only happens when you tap Export, and goes wherever you choose to share it.
- Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
