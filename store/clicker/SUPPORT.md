# Clicker Support

**Clicker can't find my TV**
- Turn the TV on and make sure your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Allow "Local Network" access: iPhone Settings → Clicker → Local Network.
- Still nothing? Find the TV's IP address on the TV (Settings → Network) and enter it under "Enter the TV's address yourself".

**The TV didn't show a code / the code didn't work**
Tap "Start over" and pick the TV again. Codes expire after a short time.

**An app doesn't open**
Some apps aren't available on every TV model or region. Open it once with the TV remote, then try again.

**Volume or power doesn't respond**
If the TV is fully off (Eco mode), it can't hear the phone. Turn on "Quick Start" in the TV's power settings.
