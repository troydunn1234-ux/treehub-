# Couch Remote Support

**Which TVs work?**
- VIZIO SmartCast TVs (2016 and newer)
- Roku TVs (TCL, Hisense, onn., Sharp, Insignia and others) and every Roku stick and box
- Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen, 2016 and newer)
- LG Smart TVs (webOS, 2014 and newer)
- Google TV and Android TV (Sony, TCL/Hisense Google TV, Philips Android TV, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield)

Your phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi. Not supported: Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, game consoles, and older TVs without built-in smart features.

**Couch Remote can't find my TV**
- Turn the TV on and make sure your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Allow "Local Network" access: iPhone Settings → Couch Remote → Local Network.
- Roku: turn on Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps → Network access.
- Still nothing? Find the TV's IP address on the TV (Settings → Network) and enter it under "Enter the TV's address yourself".

**Pairing**
- VIZIO: type the 4-digit code the TV shows. Google TV / Android TV: type the 6-character code.
- Samsung and LG: choose **Allow** on the TV when it asks.
- Roku: no code needed.
- Didn't work? Tap "Start over" and pick the TV again. Codes expire after a short time.

**An app doesn't open**
Some apps aren't available on every TV model or region. Install it on the TV, open it once with the TV remote, then try again.

**Volume or power doesn't respond**
If the TV is fully off (Eco mode), it can't hear the phone. Turn on "Quick Start" / "Fast start" / "Network standby" in the TV's power settings.
