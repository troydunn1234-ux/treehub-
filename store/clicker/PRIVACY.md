# Clankerr Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Clankerr is a remote control for your TV. It is built to collect nothing.

**What Clankerr does**
- Finds your TV on your home Wi-Fi and talks to it directly over your local network.
- Stores the pairing code your TV gives it in your iPhone's Keychain, and your chosen apps on your iPhone.

**What Clankerr does not do**
- No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No servers: nothing you do in Clankerr is sent to us or anyone else.

**Local network access**
iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. Clankerr uses it only to find and control your TV.

**Deleting your data**
Use "Forget this TV" in the app's menu, or delete the app. Everything Clankerr stored is removed.
