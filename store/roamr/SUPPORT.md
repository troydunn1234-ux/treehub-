# Roamr Support

**Trips stop recording when the phone is locked**
Allow location "Always" (Settings › Roamr › Location) and keep Precise Location on.

**Drive detection doesn't offer to record**
It needs location "Always", Motion & Fitness, and notifications. Turn it on or off in Roamr › Settings.

**Go together (group trips)**
Start a group with the people button on the Track tab, then share the invite link or the join code. Everyone needs Roamr and an iCloud account. Positions are shared only while the group is on.

**Speed limit looks wrong**
Speed limits come from OpenStreetMap. Roads without a posted limit show an estimate, marked as estimated. Always follow the posted signs.

**Heart rate is missing on a trip**
Roamr reads heart rate and workouts from Apple Health: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Roamr › Turn On All.

**Pro, subscriptions and restore**
Roamr › Settings › Roamr Pro shows the plans, Restore and Manage Subscription. Cancel anytime in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.
