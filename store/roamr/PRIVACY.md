# Roamr Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Roamr records your drives and maps them (walks or rides saved by earlier versions are kept as they were). It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

**What stays with you**
- Your trips (routes, times, distances, notes) are stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, so they follow you to a new phone. Nobody else can read them, including us.
- With your permission, Roamr reads workouts and heart rate from Apple Health. Health data never leaves your devices and is never used for advertising.
- Location is used only to record a trip you start (or to notice a drive and ask whether to record it).
- **Rootbound** (our tree-hugging app): Roamr keeps a summary of your last 60 days of trips in an App Group container on your iPhone that only our own apps can read, so Rootbound can show a walk under a tree hug. Each entry has the trip type, start and end time, distance, moving time, climb, speeds, average heart rate and a start / end point rounded to about 100 m. Routes are not shared, and nothing leaves your iPhone.

**Go together (group trips)**
When you start or join a group, your chosen name, live position, speed and heading are shared with the people in that group through Apple's iCloud, only while the group is on. Leaving or ending the group stops sharing.

**Hard-stop alert (off unless you turn it on)**
While a drive is recording, Roamr reads the phone's motion sensor to notice a sudden jolt. The emergency contact you pick (name and phone number) is saved only on your iPhone. If the alert goes off, Roamr fills in a text message with your location for you to send; it never sends anything by itself.

**Gas log**
Fill-ups you log (gallons, price, odometer, station) are stored on your iPhone and your private iCloud, like your trips. "Use where I am" takes one location fix to look up the nearest station with Apple Maps.

**Speed limits**
While you drive, Roamr downloads road and speed-limit data from public OpenStreetMap servers in map squares of about 4 km, and saves them on your phone. Those servers see which map square is being downloaded (like any map app), but nothing about you.

**What Roamr does not do**
- No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No servers of our own.

**Purchases**
Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.

**Deleting your data**
Delete a trip in History, or delete the app and its iCloud data (Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage) to remove everything.
