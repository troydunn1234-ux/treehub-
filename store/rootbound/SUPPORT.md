# Rootbound Support

**How do I log a hug?**
Tap the Hug button on Home. Add a tree photo (and a tree selfie with Pro) if you like, pin the spot (live location, from the photo, or paste a map link), tag it and save.

**How do I time a hug on my Apple Watch?**
Open Rootbound on your Watch (or tap its complication) and tap Start hug. The Watch taps your wrist, shows the time and your live heart rate, and taps again when you end it. Your iPhone then asks which tree it was ("Hug from Watch").

**How do I link my breath sessions to a hug?**
Add the tree photo: Rootbound finds every Still Pointz and mindful session within an hour of when the photo was taken and links them all. Uncheck any that don't belong. To change them later, open the hug, tap Edit hug and tap Change next to Breath sessions.

**Breathwork stats are empty**
Rootbound reads heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and mindful minutes that your Apple Watch saves to Apple Health. Allow access in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Rootbound. Watch data can take a few minutes to sync; it fills in by itself the next time you open the hug or the tree.

**Strolls stop recording when the phone is locked**
Allow location "Always" (Settings › Rootbound › Location) so a stroll keeps recording in your pocket.

**My hugs aren't on my new phone**
Sign in to the same iCloud account and keep iCloud Drive on. Syncing can take a few minutes.

**What does Rootbound Pro add?**
A one-time purchase (no subscription) that adds tree selfies, tree insights and CSV / GPX export. Everything else is free. Bought Pro on another device? Open Settings (gear on Home) › Restore Purchases.
