# Rootbound Support

**How do I log a hug?**
Tap the Hug button on Home. Add a tree photo (and a tree selfie with Pro) if you like, pin the spot (live location, from the photo, or paste a map link), tag it and save.

**How do I time a hug on my Apple Watch?**
Open Rootbound on your Watch (or tap its complication) and tap Start hug. The Watch taps your wrist, shows the time and your live heart rate, and taps again when you end it. Your iPhone then asks which tree it was ("Hug from Watch").

**How do I link my breath sessions to a hug?**
Add the tree photo: Rootbound finds every Still Pointz and mindful session within an hour of when the photo was taken and links them all. Uncheck any that don't belong. To change them later, open the hug, tap Edit hug and tap Change next to Breath sessions.

**Breathwork stats are empty**
Rootbound reads heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and mindful minutes that your Apple Watch saves to Apple Health. Allow access in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Rootbound. Watch data can take a few minutes to sync; it fills in by itself the next time you open the hug or the tree.

**How do walks show up under a hug?**
Rootbound doesn't record walks any more. Record them in Roamr (or any app that saves walks and hikes to Apple Health), then tap Sync Roamr on Home or in the hug logger. Walks within two hours of a hug show up under it as a stat (Walk · 2.3 mi · 48 min · +410 ft) and add to the tree's miles. Rootbound also syncs on its own each time you open it. To pick different walks, open the hug, tap Edit hug and tap Change walks.

**Where are the strolls I recorded in Rootbound?**
They're kept: Settings (gear on Home) › Walks › Past strolls.

**What is Nature Roll?**
Tap Nature Roll on Home (or in the Grove). Rootbound looks through your photo library on your iPhone and sorts out the nature shots: sunsets, trails, mountains, forests, water, sky and stars, wildlife and flowers. Nothing is uploaded. Pick Strict, Balanced or Everything to choose how picky it is, tap "Not nature" on any photo it got wrong, or turn a photo into a hug. It works with full or limited photo access.

**My hugs aren't on my new phone**
Sign in to the same iCloud account and keep iCloud Drive on. Syncing can take a few minutes.

**What does Rootbound Pro add?**
A one-time purchase (no subscription) that adds tree selfies, tree insights, CSV / GPX export, and the Nature Roll map and Photos albums. Everything else is free. Bought Pro on another device? Open Settings (gear on Home) › Restore Purchases.
