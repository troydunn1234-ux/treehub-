# Rootbound Support

**How do I log a hug?**
Tap the Hug button on Home. Add a tree photo (and a tree selfie with Pro) if you like, pin the spot (live location, from the photo, or paste a map link), tag it and save.

**Breathwork stats are empty**
Rootbound reads heart rate, breathing rate and mindful minutes from Apple Health around the time of the hug. Allow access in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Rootbound, and make sure your watch app saves to Apple Health.

**Strolls stop recording when the phone is locked**
Allow location "Always" (Settings › Rootbound › Location) so a stroll keeps recording in your pocket.

**My hugs aren't on my new phone**
Sign in to the same iCloud account and keep iCloud Drive on. Syncing can take a few minutes.

**What does Rootbound Pro add?**
A one-time purchase (no subscription) that adds tree selfies, tree insights and CSV / GPX export. Everything else is free. Bought Pro on another device? Open Settings (gear on Home) › Restore Purchases.
