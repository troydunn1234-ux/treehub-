# Rootbound Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

Rootbound is a tree-hugging game with strolls and hikes. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

**What stays with you**
- Your hugs (photos, tree selfies, map pins, tags, notes, how you felt) and your strolls are stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, so they follow you to a new phone. Nobody else can read them, including us.
- **Location** is used to pin the trees you hug and to record strolls and hikes you start.
- **Camera and photos** are used only for the pictures you choose to add.
- **Apple Health** (with your permission, read only): heart rate, breathing rate, mindful minutes and workouts, to fill in your breathwork stats. Health data never leaves your devices and is never used for advertising.
- Tree species suggestions are made on your iPhone by Apple's built-in image recognition. Photos are not uploaded.

**What Rootbound does not do**
- No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No servers of our own: nothing you do is sent to us.

**Purchases**
Rootbound Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

**Deleting your data**
Delete a hug in the app, or delete the app and its iCloud data (Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage) to remove everything.
