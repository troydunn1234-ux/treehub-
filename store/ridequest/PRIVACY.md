# Ride Quest Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

Ride Quest is a budget and savings game. It is built to collect nothing.

**What stays on your iPhone**
- Your goal, budgets, purchases, transfers, bills and subscriptions are stored on your iPhone only. A backup file is made only when you tap Backup, and goes wherever you choose.

**Optional bank sync (SimpleFIN Bridge)**
If you connect a bank, you sign up with SimpleFIN Bridge yourself and paste its setup token. Ride Quest exchanges it for an access key, keeps that key in the iPhone Keychain, and downloads your account balances and transactions directly from SimpleFIN to your phone. Nothing goes through our servers (we have none). Disconnect in Setup to delete the key. SimpleFIN's own privacy policy covers your account with them.

**What Ride Quest does not do**
- No accounts, sign-ups or logins of its own.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- Your financial data is never sent to us, sold or used for advertising.

**Deleting your data**
Use Setup › Danger zone to erase everything, or delete the app.
