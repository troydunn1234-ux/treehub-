# Ride Quest Support

**Getting started**
Open Setup: name your goal, set the amount and the month you want it by, and enter your balances. Then set a monthly limit for each budget category.

**Logging**
Tap Quick add to log a purchase or move money between checking, savings and cash. Moving money to savings is what levels up your quest.

**Bank sync**
Optional. Create a SimpleFIN Bridge account (a separate paid service), connect your bank there, copy the setup token and paste it in Setup › Bank sync. Purchases then log themselves; you can still edit anything.

**Backups**
Setup › Backup exports your whole save as a file. Keep one before changing phones.

**The Arcade**
Quests, boss fights and loot chests are just for fun. There is no real money, no prizes and nothing to buy.
