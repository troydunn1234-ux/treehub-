# Still Pointz Support

**The Watch doesn't tap**
Make sure the Watch isn't in silent-haptics mode (Watch Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Haptic Alerts) and keep the Still Pointz app open on the Watch during a session.

**Mindful Minutes or heart rate are missing**
iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Still Pointz › Turn On All. Heart rate during a session needs an Apple Watch.

**How does Right now pick a practice?**
It looks at the latest numbers your Apple Watch or WHOOP saves to Apple Health (heart rate, HRV, breathing rate, last night's sleep and today's workouts) and picks one practice with simple rules. Tap Why this? to see every number it used. With no Health data yet, it suggests a practice for the time of day.

**Morning and bedtime reminders**
Turn them on or off and set their times in Settings › Right now reminders. If they don't arrive, allow notifications for Still Pointz in the Settings app › Notifications.

**Is breathwork safe for me?**
The practices are gentle, but stop if you feel dizzy. If you are pregnant or have a heart, lung or blood pressure condition, check with a doctor before breath holds or sauna use.

**Pro, subscriptions and restore**
Journey › Pro (or any lock icon) shows the plans, Restore and Manage Subscription. Cancel anytime in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions at least 24 hours before the period ends.
