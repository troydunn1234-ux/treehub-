# Still Pointz Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

Still Pointz is a breathing, stillness, movement and sauna/steam guide for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is built to collect nothing.

**What stays on your devices**
- Your sessions, journey, notes, favorites and settings are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and synced between them directly.
- With your permission, Still Pointz reads heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate, sleep, steps, mindful minutes and workouts from Apple Health, to show how a session went and to pick the Right now practice. It saves your sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes (movement routines and sauna/steam sessions as workouts). Health data never leaves your devices and is never used for advertising.
- **Right now reminders** (morning and bedtime) are notifications scheduled on your iPhone; the practice in each one is picked on your iPhone.
- **Rootbound and Pulse Lab** (our other apps, if you have them): they share a short list of recent activity with Still Pointz through an App Group container on your iPhone that only our own apps can read. Still Pointz shares its recent sessions (date, practice, length and the heart-rate summary it measured) and your streak, so Rootbound can link them to tree hugs; it reads Rootbound's recent hugs (date, length, tree name and species, never where the tree is) and Pulse Lab's summary of today's numbers (like recovery, readiness, HRV and resting heart rate) for the Right now pick. Nothing is uploaded.

**What Still Pointz does not do**
- No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No servers: nothing you do in the app is sent to us or anyone else.

**Purchases**
Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.

**Deleting your data**
Delete a session in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Mindful Minutes it saved can be removed in the Health app.
