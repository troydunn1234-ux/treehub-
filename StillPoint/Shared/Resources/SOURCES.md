# Still Point — sources

Short reference list behind the practice descriptions in `practices.json`. Descriptions are paraphrased summaries for orientation, not religious instruction or medical advice.

## Classic texts and teachers

- **anapanasati-sutta** — Ānāpānasati Sutta (Majjhima Nikāya 118), mindfulness of breathing in sixteen steps. https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.118.than.html
- **satipatthana-sutta** — Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta (MN 10), the four foundations of mindfulness. https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.010.than.html
- **metta-sutta** — Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta (Sutta Nipāta 1.8), loving-kindness. https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/snp/snp.1.08.amar.html
- **ajahn-chah** — Ajahn Chah, *On Meditation* / *Clarity of Insight* (Thai Forest tradition; "Buddho" with the breath). https://www.abhayagiri.org/media/books/chah_on_meditation.epub and https://buddho.org/just-do-it/
- **goenka** — S. N. Goenka, Vipassana as taught in the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin (body-sweeping). https://www.dhamma.org/en/about/vipassana
- **dogen-fukanzazengi** — Dōgen, *Fukanzazengi* (Universal Recommendation for Zazen), Sōtō Zen. https://www.sotozen.com/eng/practice/zazen/howto/index.html
- **zen-susokukan** — Breath counting (susokukan) in Rinzai and Sōtō training; e.g. Shunryu Suzuki, *Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind*; Sōtōshū zazen guide. https://www.sotozen.com/eng/practice/zazen/howto/
- **kinhin** — Sōtōshū guide to kinhin (walking meditation between sitting periods). https://www.sotozen.com/eng/practice/zazen/howto/kinhin.html
- **chodron-tonglen** — Pema Chödrön, *Tonglen: The Path of Transformation* / "How to Practice Tonglen" (Lion's Roar). https://www.lionsroar.com/how-to-practice-tonglen/
- **nine-breaths** — Tenzin Wangyal Rinpoche, *Awakening the Sacred Body* (Bön; nine breaths of purification). https://tricycle.org/article/tibetan-yoga/
- **shamatha** — B. Alan Wallace, *The Attention Revolution* (shamatha / calm abiding). https://www.alanwallace.org/
- **hatha-yoga-pradipika** — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* (15th c., Svātmārāma), ch. 2 on prāṇāyāma incl. ujjāyī, bhrāmarī; trāṭaka among the cleansing practices.
- **light-on-pranayama** — B. K. S. Iyengar, *Light on Prāṇāyāma* (nāḍī śodhana, ujjāyī, sama vṛtti).
- **so-hum** — "Soham / Haṁsa" ajapa japa, the breath-paired mantra (e.g. Swami Muktananda, *I Am That*).
- **yoga-nidra** — Swami Satyananda Saraswati, *Yoga Nidra* (Bihar School of Yoga); also iRest (Richard Miller).
- **yoga-nidra-sleep** — Datta K, Tripathi M, Mallick HN (2017). Yoga Nidra: an innovative approach for management of chronic insomnia — a case report. *Sleep Science and Practice* 1:7. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41606-017-0009-4
- **sufi-dhikr** — Annemarie Schimmel, *Mystical Dimensions of Islam* (dhikr, breath awareness "hush dar dam" in the Naqshbandi order).
- **heart-breath** — Hazrat Inayat Khan, *The Mysticism of Sound and Music* / Sufi heart-centred breathing practices (Inayatiyya).
- **keating-centering** — Thomas Keating, *Open Mind, Open Heart*; Contemplative Outreach centering-prayer guidelines. https://www.contemplativeoutreach.org/centering-prayer-method/
- **philokalia** — *The Philokalia* (compiled 1782; St Nikodimos & St Makarios); Jesus Prayer with the breath (Hesychasm, Mount Athos). Also *The Way of a Pilgrim*.
- **lectio** — Guigo II, *Scala Claustralium* (12th c.): lectio, meditatio, oratio, contemplatio.
- **daoyin-orbit** — Mantak Chia, *Awaken Healing Energy Through the Tao* (microcosmic orbit); Livia Kohn, *Daoist Meditation*.
- **embryonic-breath** — Livia Kohn, *Chinese Healing Exercises* (taixi / embryonic breathing).
- **zhan-zhuang** — Wang Xiangzhai's zhan zhuang standing practice; Lam Kam Chuen, *The Way of Energy*.
- **shinrin-yoku** — Li, Q. (2010). Effect of forest bathing trips on human immune function. *Environ Health Prev Med* 15:9–17; Japan Forestry Agency (term coined 1982). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2793341/

## Research

- **balban-2023** — Balban MY et al. (2023). Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. *Cell Reports Medicine* 4(1):100895. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9873947
- **zaccaro-2018** — Zaccaro A et al. (2018). How breath-control can change your life: a systematic review on psycho-physiological correlates of slow breathing. *Front Hum Neurosci* 12:353. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30245619/
- **lehrer-2014** — Lehrer PM, Gevirtz R (2014). Heart rate variability biofeedback: how and why does it work? *Front Psychol* 5:756 (resonance breathing ≈ 6 breaths/min). https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00756/full
- **weitzberg-2002** — Weitzberg E, Lundberg JO (2002). Humming greatly increases nasal nitric oxide. *Am J Respir Crit Care Med* 166:144–145.
- **hofmann-2011-metta** — Hofmann SG, Grossman P, Hinton DE (2011). Loving-kindness and compassion meditation: potential for psychological interventions. *Clin Psychol Rev* 31:1126–1132.
- **goyal-2014** — Goyal M et al. (2014). Meditation programs for psychological stress and well-being: a systematic review and meta-analysis. *JAMA Intern Med* 174:357–368.
- **weil-478** — Andrew Weil, MD, "4-7-8 breath" (adapted from pranayama). https://www.drweil.com/videos-features/videos/breathing-exercises-4-7-8-breath/
- **box-breathing** — Röttger S et al. (2021). The effectiveness of combat tactical breathing as compared with prolonged exhalation. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback* 46:19–28. https://openhsu.ub.hsu-hh.de/entities/publication/16979
- **telles-nadi** — Telles S et al. (2013). Alternate-nostril yoga breathing… *Med Sci Monit Basic Res*; and Ghiya S (2017) review of nāḍī śodhana research, *Int J Yoga*.

## Movement & warm-ups

- **baduanjin** — Chinese Health Qigong Association, *Health Qigong: Ba Duan Jin* (standardised 2003); research overview e.g. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5858390 and https://www.qigonginstitute.org/abstract-print/11703
- **taichi** — Yang-style simplified 24-form (China National Sports Commission, 1956).
- **wayne-taichi** — Peter M. Wayne, *The Harvard Medical School Guide to Tai Chi* (2013).
- **rajio-taiso** — Radio calisthenics history (1928, Japan postal insurance bureau / NHK). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_calisthenics and https://www.tofugu.com/japan/rajio-taiso/
- **makko-ho** — Haruchika Nagai, *Makko-Ho: Five Minutes' Physical Fitness* (Japan Publications, 1972), describing Wataru Nagai's method.
- **surya** — Swami Satyananda Saraswati, *Asana Pranayama Mudra Bandha* (Bihar School of Yoga), Sūrya Namaskār chapter.
- **ruesi** — Thai Ruesi / Ruesi Dat Ton overview. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Ruesi
- **ruesi-watpho** — Thailand Foundation, "Exercising in (Ancient) Style: Hermit Statues of Wat Pho." https://thailandfoundation.or.th/?p=961
- **do-in** — Jacques de Langre, *The First Book of Do-In* (1971).
- **lu-jong** — Tulku Lobsang, *Lu Jong: The Power of Tibetan Yoga*; Yoga Journal, "Five Elements Tibetan Lu Jong Yoga Practice." https://www.yogajournal.com/practice/five-elements-tibetan-lu-jong-yoga-practice
